Queue

Classification reasons against the query, request and complaint definitions and illustrative examples published in a commercial bank's Grievance Redressal Policy (April 2026). The response timeline is not produced by the model: the model identifies the transaction type from a fixed list, and the timeline is looked up in code from the policy's schedule of timeframes. Interactions are constructed from that policy's illustrative examples. Portfolio prototype; not connected to any bank system, and no customer data is used.
