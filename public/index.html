OmbudsShield AI

The first pass on every customer complaint, before it enters the bank's system.

Contact an Indian bank with a problem and someone files what you said under one of three headings: a query, a request or a complaint. It takes seconds and is usually done from memory.

That choice decides what the bank owes you. A query is a question, so nothing is owed and no deadline starts. A request has a deadline. A complaint has a deadline, an acknowledgement, and a review.

So a complaint filed as a query disappears. The Governor of the Reserve Bank of India has called this a gross regulatory violation, not a clerical slip.

OmbudsShield AI is for the officer making that call. It reads what the customer said and tells her which of the three it is. It shows her where the policy says so. It works out the deadline. When the wording could go either way, it says so and lets her decide.

The classification is real.
Every case is read by a language model at the moment you click, against the bank's published definitions. Nothing is pre-classified or keyword-matched, so anything you type will be classified on its own terms — including wording that has never been seen before.
